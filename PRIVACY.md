# Privacy policy

MirrorDL collects no personal data. It has no account, no tracking, no analytics and no ads.

It connects only to:

- **the download links you give it**, to check them and download the files;
- **web pages you paste a link to**, to look for the links to files on them (Settings: "Look for
  download links on web pages");
- **pages you open in MirrorDL's browser**, like any browser does (Settings: "Open download pages in
  MirrorDL's browser");
- **api.github.com**, once when the window app starts, to see whether a newer version exists. Only
  the version from GitHub does this, and it can be switched off in Settings; the Microsoft Store
  version gets its updates from the Store. Like any website, GitHub receives the usual request
  data, such as your IP address.

**Click'n'Load:** the window app listens on 127.0.0.1, port 9666, for links that link sites send
from your browser. Only programs on your own computer can reach it. Links that arrive are shown to
you, never downloaded by themselves. It can be switched off in Settings.

**Clipboard:** only if you switch on "Watch the clipboard for links", MirrorDL looks at what you
copy and takes the links from it. Nothing of it leaves your computer.

**What stays on your computer:** the files you download and MirrorDL's progress files next to
them; and, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MirrorDL`, the settings, the download list (so that paused downloads
are still there after a restart), the history of finished downloads, and the cookies and cache of
MirrorDL's browser. Each can be switched off or cleared in Settings. MirrorDL sends none of it
anywhere.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/Nawid3333/MirrorDL/issues).
